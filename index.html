Always check the pack label and use a meat thermometer. Push the probe into the thickest part, between leg and breast: aim for 75°C or more, with no pink meat and clear juices. The Food Standards Agency also lists 70°C held for 2 minutes. Never wash the turkey. These times are estimates and ovens vary; if your pack gives different timings, follow the pack.

The turkey timings follow published UK guidance (Food Standards Agency, BBC Good Food); where sources differ the calculator uses the longer figure. Oven preheat, carving, spare time and side-dish start times are our own allowances, not official guidance: change the lists near the top of the script to suit your recipes.
